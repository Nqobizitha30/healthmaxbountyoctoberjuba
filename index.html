GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

A little clarity goes a long way.

Here’s what to know before you get started.

What is Champion Health Coverage?

Champion Health Coverage is a one-stop shop for people looking for affordable health insurance. Start here to explore coverage that may fit your needs and budget.

What do I need to get started?

Just your name and email address. You don’t need to enter medical information or payment details to start your inquiry.

Does clicking the coverage link enroll me in a plan?

No. The link takes you to the offer page. Clicking it does not enroll you in a health plan or guarantee coverage.

Can I get health insurance in 2026?

Your enrollment options depend on the plan, your state, and your eligibility. Some plans have specific enrollment periods; a qualifying life event may make other enrollment options available.
